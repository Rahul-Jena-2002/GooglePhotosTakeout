The Problem

Why Your Photos Have Wrong Dates

When you export from Google Photos, your memories get corrupted. We fix that.

😢
Without GT Metadata Merger
Google strips EXIF "Date Taken" from your exported files and stores them in hidden .json sidecars. When you open the photos, they all appear as today's date. Your memories are completely out of order in any photo viewer, gallery app, or file manager.
🎉
With GT Metadata Merger
GT Metadata Merger intelligently reads every .json file, matches it to your photo (even handling Google's truncated filenames), and injects the correct original date back into the EXIF header of the photo binary itself. Your photos will be perfectly sorted in any app forever.
Features

Everything You Need

Built for speed, privacy, and reliability. No compromises.

🔒
100% Local & Private
Your photos never leave your device. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. Everything runs entirely on your own computer.
⚡
Java 21 Virtual Threads
Powered by Project Loom, the engine processes hundreds of photos simultaneously. What used to take hours now takes minutes.
🖼️
Deep EXIF Injection
Goes beyond file system dates. Embeds the date permanently into the binary EXIF header of JPEG images — works in every app on every OS.
🎬
MP4 & MOV Support
Rewrites the internal creation_time metadata boxes inside MP4 and MOV video files. Your videos will finally show the right date too.
🤖
Dynamic JSON Matching
Automatically handles Google's weird truncated filenames like IMG_2023.jpg.supplem.json using a dynamic regex engine.
💻
No Installation Needed
Download, unzip, and run. No Java, no Node.js, no dependencies. Bundled as a native executable for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

How It Works

Simple as 1, 2, 3

No technical knowledge required. Just point, click, and done.

Download & Unzip
Download the executable for your OS from the Releases page. Unzip and run — no installation wizard, no admin rights needed.
Select Your Folders
Use the native folder browser to pick your Google Takeout folder as input, and an empty folder as output.
Configure Options
Optionally set the Takeout export date to filter bad timestamps. Enable "Deep EXIF Injection" to embed dates inside the files themselves.
Click Start & Relax
The engine processes all your files in parallel using Virtual Threads. Watch the live log stream as your memories are fully restored!
